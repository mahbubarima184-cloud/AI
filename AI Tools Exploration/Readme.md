1. Short Video Creation :
The video covers the AI course material, specifically combining the theory (topics covered in the subject) and the lab tasks (Algorithms and Games).
🛠 Tool Used
 * Tool Name: InVideo AI (invideo.io)
📄 Steps Taken
 * Script Generation: Developed a comprehensive script outlining the key theoretical concepts and corresponding lab   tasks/algorithms from the AI course. This included sections on machine learning, specific algorithms (like decision trees or reinforcement learning), and the games explored in lab sessions.
 * Tool Selection & Input: Logged into InVideo AI. Selected a relevant template and input the prepared script into the platform's text-to-video function.
 * Content Synthesis: The AI tool automatically generated a video draft, selecting relevant stock footage, images, and background music to match the narrative and keywords in the script.
 * Review and Editing: Reviewed the draft for accuracy and flow. Made minor manual adjustments to the visuals, text overlays, and pacing to ensure the minimum one-minute duration and clarity.
 * Final Export: Rendered and downloaded the final video file for submission.
